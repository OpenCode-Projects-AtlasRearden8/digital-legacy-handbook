# The Handoff Letter (printable, one page)

*This is the most important page in the folder: one handwritten-or-typed,
signed page that tells your person everything at a glance. Fill it out slowly
if you need to — but fill it out. Print this page using the Print button in
the built site, or copy the template below by hand.*

---

## How to fill it in

- Write it for **one** trusted person (they can share further).
- No passwords here — only **where things live**. (If you truly must pass a
  passcode this way, put a sealed envelope behind this letter instead.)
- Keep it under one page. It should be readable in five minutes in a hallway.
- Date it and sign it. Update the date when you review it each year.

## The letter (print or copy this)

---

**IF YOU ARE READING THIS, I HAVE EITHER DIED OR AM UNABLE TO ACT FOR
MYSELF — and I trusted you with this. I'm sorry for the weight of the day.
This letter plus the folder behind it is everything practical; the people who
love you are everything else.**

**My details:**
Name: ______________________________
Date this letter was last updated: ______________
Signature: ________________________

**This folder lives at:** (drawer? safe? whose house? city?)
________________________________________________________________

**The digital copy** (if any): an encrypted file at
______________________________ — password is/was passed to
__________________ separately, by voice/in person, on ____ / ____ / ______.

**First three things to do:**
1. My phone: passcode location is
   ______________________________________________
2. My primary email account is (address, provider):
   ______________________________________________ — where its password lives:
   ______________________________________________
3. My bank accounts (institution, joint or solo only):
   ______________________________________________

**People to call, in order:**
1. ________________________________________
2. ________________________________________
3. ________________________________________

**Wishes, short version:**
Social accounts:  ______________________________
(memorialize / delete / contact names)
Donate or keep any items, if specified:
______________________________________________
Anything I especially wanted you to know:
______________________________________________

**What is NOT in this folder:** my passwords (they're where noted inside the
folder), anything sealed and marked "separate location," and my real feelings
— for those, I hope my life already spoke.

*Thank you. I spent some care on you. — ________________________*

---

## Notes on the letter

- **"Passcode location" beats copying the passcode** onto the letter: write
  "password manager entry 'Phone'", or "in the sealed envelope behind this
  page", or "in my wallet's card file." Everywhere a secret would live on
  paper in the open, prefer a pointer.
- Update the **date** at the top at the annual review; the letter is
  intentionally the only page that needs to change most years.
- Two signed copies are not paranoia: one in the folder, one with your
  attorney or a second trusted person. Keep them identical.

## Printing

In the built site, open this page and use your browser's Print function — the
handoff-letter page has dedicated print styling so it fits one clean page
(centers margins, hides navigation, keeps the letter rows together). Or just
hand-write it; handwriting is completely acceptable and arguably warmer.
